## Youth Guarantee

The Youth Guarantee applies to people aged 16 to 30 who need help from Nav to find work or complete their education.


### Contents on this page


### Contents on this page

The Youth Guarantee means you will be offered a dedicated contact person at Nav who will provide personalised support and guidance based on your needs. You may be offered:

- Help with applying for jobs and connecting with employers
- Completing subjects from upper secondary school (high school)
- Assessment of education options and need for additional work qualification
- Guidance and advice on managing your finances
- Support in coordinating with child welfare services, health services, education, or other relevant agencies
- Workplace adjustments in cooperation with your employer
