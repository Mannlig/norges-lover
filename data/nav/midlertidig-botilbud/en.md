## Temporary (emergency) accommodation

### Contents on this page

You can get help from Nav to find a temporary accommodation if you do not have anywhere to sleep and stay for the next 24 hours. Nav is obliged to find you temporary accommodation if you cannot do it on your own.

You can also get help from Nav to find a temporary accommodation if you temporarily stay at your family or friends' place.

What is offered as temporary accommodation varies from municipality to municipality.

The Nav office must ensure that the temporary accommodation offer is suitable for your needs, and that the housing has a proper standard.
