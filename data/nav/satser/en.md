#### Assistance allowance

Financial support for children or adults who need long-term, private care and supervision.

#### Attendance allowance for sick children

Ensures you income during absence from work when you need to care for a child who has been admitted or is undergoing treatment in hospital.


#### Attendance allowance in the final phase of life

#### Basic pattern and stitching of clothing

#### Benefit for former family caregivers

#### Car support, special equipment, and grants for vehicles

Provides financial support for vehicles, vehicle adaptations, and special equipment for cars.


#### Cash-for-care benefit

#### Child benefit

Financial support when you have children under age 18.


#### Child care benefit for single parents

Covers part of the cost for kindergarten, after-school program (SFO) or daycare when you have sole care of a child and are working.

#### Child support

#### Children’s pension

#### Disability benefit

#### Sickness benefit

Support for childcare and expenses you have in connection with education or job search.

#### Supplementary benefit for persons over 67 who have only lived a short period in Norway

Ensures that you are paid wages, holiday pay and other payment for work you are due, when your employer is unable to pay.
