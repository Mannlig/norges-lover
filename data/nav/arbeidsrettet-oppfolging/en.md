## How to get occupational follow-up

When you contact Nav for help finding a job or keeping the job you have, you are entitled to an assessment of your job opportunities and of the types of help and support that can be relevant for you.


### Contents on this page


### Contents on this page

Everyone who registers as a job seeker will be assessed to see if they need occupational follow-up. You can also receive occupational follow-up from Nav even if you do not register as a job seeker. Nav will then assess your need for follow-up in accordance with Section 14a of the Nav Act.


#### We can help with

assessment of your capacity for work

information about opportunities and adaptation

advice and guidance to keep a job

employment schemes or training courses

you will be assigned a Nav counsellor

The services you get is tailored to your situation and may change if your needs change.

You should ask for occupational follow-up if you need help finding or keeping a job, but are not applying for new jobs right now

This applies to you if you:

- are a student and you need adaptation in order to complete your education
- are not applying for any form financial support from Nav that requires you to register as a job seeker
- need help finding or keeping a job, but you are not ready to register as a job seeker
Your health does not have to be affected to get occupational follow-up.


#### When should you not ask for occupational follow-up?

If you are actively seeking a job, you should instead register as a job seeker. This is especially relevant if:

- You may be entitled to financial support that requires you to register as a job seeker, e.g. unemployment benefit (link to unemployment benefit page)
- You have been laid off
- You are able to accept employment on short notice
- You want Nav to be able to match you with job vacancies

#### Are you uncertain about what would be most appropriate for your situation?

Are you unsure whether you should register as a job seeker, or whether you should only request occupational follow-up?

Answer a few short questions and receive a recommendation based on your situation.
