## Apply for approval of paternity or co-maternity established abroad

Paternity or co-maternity established abroad may be approved in Norway.


### Contents on this page


### Contents on this page

If the paternity or co-maternity is approved in Norway, the father or co-mother will have the same rights and obligations as if the parenthood was established in Norway.

These rights and obligations will apply in all contexts where paternity or co-maternity matters.

If paternity or co-maternity for your child has been established outside of the Nordic countries, you may apply for the same approval in Norway.

You may also apply for the approval of foreign decisions about changes in paternity or release of paternity.

#### How to apply

Use this form if you need to have a foreign decision regarding paternity or co-maternity approved in Norway.

Fill out the form and

- Your child’s birth certificate. If you can, submit an international or extended version, which shows how the paternity or co-maternity was established.
- If the birth certificate does not contain information on how the paternity or co-maternity was established, you need to submit extra documentation explaining this, such as a declaration, a court order or an administrative decision.
The documents must be in their original form. If you do not want to send your original documents, you may send documents that has been confirmed as copies by foreign authorities.

The birth certificate or the documentation on the establishment of the paternity or co-maternity need to have an affixed apostille stamp or to be legalised by the country’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Documents in other languages must be translated to Norwegian or English by a state authorised translator.


#### Processing time for applications

The processing time is the time from when we receive your application until we have made a decision. Remember that we need all the necessary documentation to process your application.


#### Processing time for complaints

Have you received a decision from us that you believe is incorrect? You can appeal to the Nav unit that issued the decision. They will reassess your case. If they do not agree with your appeal, they will forward it to the Ministry of Children and Families.


#### When your application has been processed

If the paternity or co-maternity is approved, Nav will submit the decision to the Norwegian Tax Administration (Skatteetaten) for registration.
