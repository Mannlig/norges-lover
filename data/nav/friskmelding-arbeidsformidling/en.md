## Fit for employment scheme

If all possibilities of returning to your workplace have been tried, you can receive sickness benefit for up to 12 weeks while you look for a new job.

This scheme is applicable if your health is such that you can return to work, but not to the job you are on sick leave from.

The scheme applies to everyone who was working at the time they went on sick leave and who receives sickness benefits from Nav. Both employees, self-employed and freelancers can be entitled to the scheme.

You can receive sickness benefit for up to 52 weeks. That includes the weeks you receive sickness benefit through the fit for employment scheme.

If you have less than 12 weeks of the sickness benefit, the period you can receive sickness benefit through the fit for employment scheme will be shorter.

To use the scheme “fit for employment”, you must do the following:

- Initiate the scheme. Both you, the one who has put you on sick leave, and Nav can do this. You can initiate the scheme by contacting your advisor at the Nav office.
- Confirm with Nav that you can use the scheme. This must be done regardless of who initiates the scheme.
- Resign from your job, and your employer must give you written confirmation of this. If you have a notice period, you must also get confirmation from your employer that you are released from working during the notice period.
Receive a decision from Nav that you can use the scheme “fit for employment”.

It is important that you do this in the correct order, so you don't risk losing your income.

To receive sickness benefit as declared for fit for employment scheme, you must submit an application for sickness benefit every 14 days. You will see when the applications should be sent after you have received a decision on being declared fit for employment scheme. We need the information from the application to calculate how much you should be paid.
