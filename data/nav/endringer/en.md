## Give Nav correct information

When you apply for financial support or services from Nav, it is important that you provide correct information. This helps us give you the support you are entitled to, at the right time.


### Contents on this page


### Contents on this page

Nav relies on correct information from you to process your case as quickly as possible and to make accurate decisions and payments.


#### You are required to provide correct information to Nav

You are required to provide Nav with correct and complete information. This obligation also applies to any changes that occur after you have submitted your application.


#### Consequences of providing incorrect information

If you give wrong or incomplete information, it can lead to:

- Longer processing times
- Incorrect decisions, such as payments that are too low or for too short a period
- Payments from Nav that are too high and must be repaid
- In the most serious cases, being reported to the police. This does not apply in cases where we ask for estimates or other uncertain information.
To receive the correct payment, you must also report any changes to your income or life situation.


#### How to report changes

Let us know immediately if

- there are changes in your income, family situation, or employment situation
- you plan to stay or move abroad

#### Situations that may affect your payment

Typical situations that may affect your payment include:

- Changes in your or your partner’s income
- Changes in marital status (marriage, cohabitation, separation, death)
- Changes in family situation (having children, children moving out, no longer supporting children)
- Changes in employment (new job, increased hours, or income changes)
- Staying or moving abroad
