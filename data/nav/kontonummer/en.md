## Add or change bank account number

Make sure the correct account number is registered when you apply for financial support from Nav. You can see and change your account number by logging in to nav.no.

### Contents on this page

If you have a bank account, or can get one, we recommend registering your account number with Nav. That way, your money will be transferred safely straight into your account.

Nav prepares the payment well in advance of the payment date for some financial benefits. If you want to change your account number, we recommend doing it a week before the next payment.

You may only have one account number registered with Nav as a private individual. The account number is also used for payments that Nav makes on behalf of The Norwegian Public Service Pension Fund (Statens pensjonskasse).

If you log in to nav.no with BankID, Buypass or Comfides, you can change your account number in "Personal data".


#### Payment to a foreign bank account

Nav usually makes payments to Norwegian bank accounts. If you do not have a Norwegian bank account, you can register an account number from a foreign bank.

Please note that there is a fee for each payment sent abroad. It may also take longer for the money to reach your account. For some types of financial support, you must also be resident in Norway.

#### Add or change Norwegian bank account

To add or change a Norwegian bank account number by post, you use the form called "Notification of new bank account number".


#### Payments to a foreign bank account

If you want your payments to be made to a foreign bank account, and you want to send the form by post, you must download a bank information form.

Valid identification is an ID card that contains date of birth, name, signature and photo, for example passport, driver's license, National ID card. You can also show valid ID in person at a Nav office.

If you do not have, or are unable to get, a bank account, you may request a payment card from Nav. Please contact your local Nav office or call us at 55 55 33 33.


#### Using the payment card

The payment card can be used within Norway to purchase goods and services in shops, and to withdraw cash from shops and ATMs/cash machines. You can check your balance at an ATM/cash machine.

The card and PIN code will be sent separately. The payment card is personal. Please take care to keep both the card and the PIN code secure. The card is valid for three years.
